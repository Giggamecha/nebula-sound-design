The sound job

Good gameplay sounds.
Just the essentials.

Hits. Holds. Misses. Shape changes.

Easy to hear over the music. Satisfying to play. Not harsh or tiring.

These are the old sounds, not the finished replacements. The buttons explain the job. They do not set the number of new files to make.

What matters most

The hits should be clear in quiet songs and busy songs. They should still feel good after hearing them hundreds of times. The music should stay enjoyable, not get buried.

For the designer: Send examples of your work, a price, and your availability. Deliver WAV files that can be used in a commercial game.
